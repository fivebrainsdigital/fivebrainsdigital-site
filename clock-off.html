Questions people ask

How do I paste ChatGPT into Google Docs without losing the formatting?

On a computer, open Google Docs, go to Tools, then Preferences, and tick "Enable Markdown." Then right-click where you want the text and choose "Paste from Markdown." Headings and bold should come through as real formatting - check it the first time. The free guide above walks you through it.

Why does AI text show up full of stars and hash marks?

The AI marks formatting with a shorthand called Markdown - two stars for bold, a hash mark for a heading. Some programs turn that into real formatting. Word, most email and plain website boxes usually don't, so you see the symbols. A chat window can't see where you're going to paste, so it guesses.

How do I get AI text into Word without the symbols?

Word on Windows and Mac has no Markdown setting, and Word in a web browser only converts some of it. The simplest fix is to tell the AI before it writes: plain text only, no stars, no hash marks. The free guide has the exact words to paste in.
