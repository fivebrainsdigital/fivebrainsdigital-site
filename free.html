Questions people ask

Are these guides really free?

Yes. The guides need no email address and no account, and there is nothing to unsubscribe from. Download them and print them. Admitted, or Observation? can also be read online. The Scam Sheet is different: it is a free newsletter, so getting it by email means signing up with your email address.

Can I print copies for my church, library or support group?

Yes. Print as many as you want and leave them where people can take one. That is what they are for. Please hand them out free and do not change them. Each guide has its check date on the front - before you print a new batch, download the current copy, because figures like Medicare premiums and deductibles are updated every year, starting in January.

Are the guides easy to read?

They are written in plain English. In the PDF of Admitted, or Observation?, the main text is set in 16-point type. The boxed notes and the source list are smaller.

How do I know the facts are current?

Each guide lists its sources, who published them, and the date we checked them. Rules and prices can change after that date, so check the source before you rely on a number that matters. If you find something that has changed, write to hello@fivebrainsdigital.com so we can check it and fix the guide.
