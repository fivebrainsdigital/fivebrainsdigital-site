Guides for the things nobody warned you about

Somebody's parent goes into hospital and the paperwork starts. Somebody gets a phone call about a diagnosis. Somebody gets an email that turns out to be a scam. There is usually nobody to ask, and everything written about it is either a brochure or a legal document.

p>

Five Brains Digital writes the version in between. Every guide is set in 16-point type, written in ordinary English, and every fact in it names the body that published it and the date it was checked. No fluff, no jargon, no advice about your particular situation - the published rules, plainly, and the questions worth asking.

Start with a free one

Admitted, or Observation?

Free, 29 pages, read it here or download the PDF

A person can spend four nights in a hospital bed and still not count as "admitted" - and that one word decides whether Medicare pays for the nursing home afterwards. Most families find out weeks later, from a bill. This covers the two statuses, what each costs, the three notices the hospital owes you, the appeal that did not exist before 2025, and the review-organisation phone number for every state.
