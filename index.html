Questions people ask

What is Five Brains Digital?

A small publisher of plain-language guides for the things nobody warned you about: a parent's hospital stay, Medicare paperwork, dementia, estates and AI tools at work, plus a free newsletter on current scams. The Gray Age guides are set in 16-point type.

Who are the guides for?

Mostly families caring for an older parent, and older adults handling it themselves. The Scam Sheet is written for the people being targeted and the families who worry about them. Clock Off is for people at work.

Are any guides free?

Yes. "Admitted, or Observation?" (read it online or download it) and "Stop Retyping the AI" are free, with no email and no account. The Scam Sheet newsletter is free to read too, and it will stay free. Getting it by email takes your email address.

Is this advice for my situation?

No. Everything here is general information, not legal, medical, financial or tax advice. For your own situation, talk to a licensed professional. We cannot tell you whether a message you received is a scam, or diagnose anything.

How do I report a mistake?

Write to hello@fivebrainsdigital.com. We will look into it, and if something is wrong or has changed, we will correct the guide.
